MIKE VRABEL MAKES A SHOCKING STATEMENT BEFORE WEEK 9!

Patriots Nation, Mike Vrabel just stepped to the podium ahead of the Packers game, and he had some of the most direct answers we’ve heard from him all season.

A national report said Christian Barmore and Carlton Davis were available ahead of the trade deadline. And Vrabel shut it down on the spot. He said he hasn’t shared that information with anybody. And that none of those conversations have happened with him.

But that wasn’t the only news. Vrabel also revealed that two players are returning to practice from injured reserve. He gave an update on A.J. Brown’s knee. He revealed that two key players are out with illness. And he gave a scouting report on the Packers that should have every Patriots fan locked in for Sunday.

Let’s start with the biggest moment of the press conference. The trade rumors.

A reporter brought up a national report saying Christian Barmore and Carlton Davis were available as the Patriots approach the trade deadline. And the reporter pointed out it had sparked a lot of discussion about a 5 and 3 team that’s right in the middle of the AFC race.

And Vrabel didn’t dance around it.

He said sometimes national reports have a tendency to do that. And then he said it directly. He has not shared that information with anybody.

He said this time of year, all these things get drummed up. And he said the team is focused on preparing for Green Bay, and making the corrections from the last game. He called it business as normal. And he said he doesn’t know where a lot of these things come from, but they certainly didn’t come from any conversations with him.

Patriots Nation, that’s about as clear as a head coach gets in the middle of trade season.

Now, does that mean Barmore and Davis will definitely stay? No coach can promise that. The deadline is Tuesday, and front offices make calls all the time. But it tells you Vrabel isn’t the one shopping these players. And it tells you the locker room isn’t being distracted by the noise.

Then a reporter asked if Vrabel felt he needed to clear the air with the players involved in those rumors.

And his answer told us exactly how he runs this team. He said what he feels compelled to do every day is coach the football team. Get the guys prepared. And at this point in the season, find the small things they can improve on.

And then he gave us a look at the actual conversations he’s having with those two players. And this was great.

About Barmore, Vrabel said Christian got a sack the other day, against Miami, using the same move he’s been working on. He cleared his feet. He cleared the trash from the center and the guard. And Vrabel told him, let’s keep working on this. He said there are still a couple of times where Barmore’s feet are getting tangled up inside, but he’s clearing and getting to the edge of a blocker.

And about Davis, Vrabel said he told him he did a good job replacing in the run game. And then they talked about being mindful of his technique, whether it’s zone, man, or a combo call against a bunch formation.

Vrabel said those are the conversations he tries to have with all the players. And he’s asked the coaches to do the same. Eight games into the season, with practice reps going down, they’re still finding ways to improve.

Think about what that means. The head coach isn’t talking to Barmore about trades. He’s talking to him about clearing his feet on a pass rush move. He’s not talking to Davis about his future. He’s talking to him about run-fit technique. That’s a coach who sees both of them as part of this team right now.

And remember the numbers. Barmore’s snaps have dropped this season, to about 41 percent. But his pressure rate is still 9.3 percent, and his pass rush grade is close to 80. And he just got a sack in Miami.

And Davis? In the Miami game, he played 97 percent of the defensive snaps until he was rested in the fourth quarter. And the moment he left, the backup gave up a touchdown on the very next drive. That’s how important he is.

Now, does that mean the Patriots won’t make any moves? Not at all.

A reporter asked if the Patriots are willing to trade players at the deadline if the situation is right. And Vrabel said, as he’s said many times, that he’s sure Eliot Wolf and anyone else who speaks for the organization would say the same thing. They’ll always look for ways to make the roster better and make the team better. And that could happen in multiple ways.

And he pointed to something that’s been a big factor all season. The roster has had a lot of moving parts. Guys coming off the roster, trying to get them onto the practice squad. Guys coming back from injured reserve with return designations. And Vrabel said they only get so many of those spots. So there’s a lot they have to look at with the roster.

Remember, the Patriots only have a limited number of IR return slots left. That’s why they released punter Bryce Baringer earlier this season. Every one of those slots is precious.

And another reporter pushed a little further. Can you make this year’s team better by trading away players who contribute?

And Vrabel’s answer was really smart. He said he doesn’t know. He said there are probably a bunch of ways to make this team better. Coach better. Execute better. Maybe players who are available on the street. There are multiple ways. And he said they have those conversations every day.

And then he pointed out the schedule. There’s another week before the trade deadline. And the team will be leaving for Munich next week. So right now, the focus is on today. How they get better. How they run the ball better. How they affect the quarterback. All the things that, if they don’t do them, it’s going to be tough.

That’s Vrabel. One game at a time. Always.

Now let’s get to the injury news. Because there was a lot of it.

A.J. Brown won’t practice. Vrabel said he doesn’t think you’ll see Brown at practice. And when he was asked if injured reserve is a possibility for Brown, Vrabel said he can only speak about today, and where Brown is today, and what they feel he can do today. That’s why he won’t be out there.

And when he was asked about his concern level with Brown’s knee, Vrabel said his concern is about preparing for the Packers. That’s his only concern. Figuring out what they do on first and second down, how they practice, and how they carry over the second half of last week’s game, where the team looked closer to what Vrabel expects.

Now, that’s not great news on Brown. He missed the Miami game with knee and ankle issues. He’s played in only three games this season, with nine catches for 101 yards. And the IR question being asked at all tells you this could be more than a short-term thing. But Vrabel isn’t ruling anything in or out.

And there’s more. Christian Barmore and TreVeyon Henderson are both away from the team with an illness. So they won’t practice either. Remember, Henderson also missed the Miami game because of an illness. So that’s something to keep an eye on.

But here’s the good news. And this is big.

Mack Hollins and Greg Van Roten are being designated to return from injured reserve. Vrabel said they’ll come back to practice. He said the team wants to get those guys out there, because there’s only so much the trainers and strength coaches can do with them. So hopefully they can work those guys back in and see where they are as the week goes on.

Patriots Nation, that’s huge.

Think about Hollins. He had 209 receiving yards before going on injured reserve with a calf injury. He brings a physical presence to routes and blocking. With A.J. Brown out, and Romeo Doubs on injured reserve, getting Hollins back would be a massive boost for a thin receiver room.

And think about Van Roten. The offensive line has been the biggest problem all season. Mike Onwenu is out for the season. Alijah Vera-Tucker is on injured reserve. The Patriots used 14 different offensive line combinations through six games. Getting Van Roten back gives this line another veteran body, and more stability in front of Drake Maye.

Smash that like button if you’re excited to see Mack Hollins and Greg Van Roten back on the practice field.

And Vrabel also gave an update on Harold Landry. A reporter asked if he’s still tracking to return at some point.

Vrabel said anybody who’s still with the team and getting treatment is tracking. How quickly, he doesn’t know. But he said Landry is working extremely hard. He’s doing some things on the side, working with the strength coaches and the trainers. And whenever he’s ready, he’ll start the return process.

So no timeline. But Landry is getting closer. And when he’s back, a pass rush that already has 22 sacks, seventh in the league, gets even more dangerous.

Now let’s talk about Carlton Davis one more time. A reporter asked if Davis has played as well as he did last year, when he’s been available.

And Vrabel said midway through the season isn’t the time for him to give a state of the union on each player. But he said he loves CD’s toughness, his ability, and the way he competes.

And then he turned it into a message about the whole defense. He said the defense has to improve. He said the way they gave up explosive plays last week, and let Miami drive the ball, wasn’t the standard. And the big focus is creating more turnovers on defense.

And he said he doesn’t believe in breaking down each player after eight games. He doesn’t think that’s fair to anybody.

That’s Vrabel protecting his players, while holding the whole unit to a high standard. And creating turnovers is a big deal. Remember, the Patriots hadn’t won the turnover battle in any game this season heading into Miami.

Patriots Floor family, quick question. What does this defense need most against Green Bay? More pressure, fewer explosive plays, or more turnovers?

Now let’s get to the Packers. Because Vrabel’s scouting report was eye-opening.

He said nobody has had more 20-yard pass plays than Green Bay. And he called their quarterback, Jordan Love, wildly efficient. He said Love has a few off days, but they’re few and far between.

He said the Packers throw shots down the field. They have great speed on the edges. An excellent tight end in Tucker Kraft. And they’re getting Josh Jacobs back, and it seems like they’re trying to work him in more, especially in the red zone.

And Vrabel said the Packers have protected better over the last few games. He said when your quarterback is one of your best players, protecting him becomes a big emphasis. Because when you give Love time, he finds his receivers. Matthew Golden. Christian Watson. Tucker Kraft.

And on defense, Vrabel called the Packers very physical. He said they’ve created the second-most negative plays in the league. And 17 percent of their opponents’ drives end in a turnover. That leads the league.

Think about that, Patriots Nation. A team that creates turnovers on 17 percent of drives. And a Patriots team that’s struggled with turnovers all season. That’s the matchup to watch on Sunday.

The good news? Drake Maye has been protecting the ball beautifully lately. In Miami, he went 16 of 20 for 199 yards, two passing touchdowns, a rushing touchdown, and zero interceptions. He’s put up at least three total touchdowns in five straight games. If he keeps taking care of the ball against this Packers defense, the Patriots have a real shot.

Smash that like button if you believe Drake Maye outplays Jordan Love on Sunday.

And Vrabel had a lot of respect for Jordan Love. A reporter mentioned that Packers head coach Matt LaFleur said before the season that Love is the best quarterback he’s ever had at the line of scrimmage. Which is quite a statement, considering LaFleur also coached Aaron Rodgers.

And Vrabel said you can see it on tape. You see it in protection on third down. Whether Love is adjusting and getting the ball out quickly, or changing the protection and making the call, communicating with his line. Vrabel said you’ve seen that growth over the last couple of years.

And Vrabel gave Love a lot of credit for the way his career started. Sitting and watching. He said that’s hard. It’s hard to develop when you’re not playing. But Love did it. And he probably learned a lot from watching Aaron Rodgers, and from working with LaFleur.

So this is a quarterback who’s grown into a star. And the Patriots’ pass rush, with Elijah Ponder, Gabe Jacas, Milton Williams, and Barmore if he’s healthy, has to make him uncomfortable. If Love gets time, he will find Golden, Watson, and Kraft.

So let’s put it all together.

The shocking statement: Vrabel shutting down the national report that Barmore and Davis are available, saying he hasn’t shared that information with anybody, and calling it business as normal.

The conversations he is having: coaching Barmore on clearing his feet after his sack in Miami, and praising Davis for his work in the run game.

The good news: Mack Hollins and Greg Van Roten returning to practice from injured reserve, giving the receivers and the offensive line a much-needed boost. And Harold Landry still working hard toward a return.

The concerns: A.J. Brown not practicing, with the IR question being asked. And Barmore and TreVeyon Henderson away with an illness.

And the challenge: a Packers team with the most 20-yard pass plays in the league, a wildly efficient Jordan Love, and a defense that forces turnovers on 17 percent of drives, the most in the NFL.

The Patriots are 5 and 3, and 3 and 0 in the division. They’re coming off a comeback win in Miami. Then it’s the trade deadline on Tuesday, and Munich the week after.
